Hemming a finished garment

Confirmed Skill 5 of 6 in stage 5 · Tunde Adeyemi

What this skill involves

Folding and stitching a clean, even edge on a finished garment so it holds its shape and sits correctly when worn. The hem is the last thing done to a piece and the first thing a customer notices, so it is judged on evenness of depth, invisibility of stitching from the right side, and how the garment hangs once it is on the body.

To be confirmed at this stage the apprentice must hem three garments in different weights of cloth — a light cotton, a wool blend, and a lined piece — without the stitching showing through and without the hem pulling.
