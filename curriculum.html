The curriculum

Six stages and thirty-one skills, from the first straight seam to a finished piece ready to leave the shop. Every workshop in the programme teaches to this.

See who is where
Stage 1 — Sewing fundamentals 5 skills +
  • Threading and tensioning the machine Skill 1
  • A straight seam at constant width Skill 2
  • Backstitching and securing a seam Skill 3
  • Hand basting Skill 4
  • Changing a needle and winding a bobbin Skill 5
Stage 2 — Fabric preparation 5 skills +
  • Identifying fibre and weave Skill 1
  • Pre-shrinking and pressing cloth Skill 2
  • Finding and following the grain line Skill 3
  • Laying up and pinning Skill 4
  • Marking with tailor's chalk Skill 5
Stage 3 — Measurement & cutting 5 skills +
  • Taking body measurements Skill 1
  • Reading and adjusting a size chart Skill 2
  • Adding seam and hem allowance Skill 3
  • Cutting on the grain Skill 4
  • Notching and marking pieces Skill 5
Stage 4 — Pattern drafting 5 skills +
  • Drafting a basic bodice block Skill 1
  • Dart manipulation Skill 2
  • Truing seam lines Skill 3
  • Grading between sizes Skill 4
  • Drafting a sleeve cap Skill 5
Stage 5 — Garment construction 6 skills +
  • Attaching a lined waistband Skill 1
  • Welt pockets on a jacket front Skill 2
  • Setting a two-piece sleeve Skill 3
  • Interfacing a collar stand Skill 4
  • Hemming a finished garment Skill 5
  • Hand-finishing a jacket lining Skill 6
Stage 6 — Finishing & quality 5 skills +
  • Pressing and blocking Skill 1
  • Machine buttonholes Skill 2
  • Hand-sewn buttons and shanks Skill 3
  • Final inspection and measurement check Skill 4
  • Packing and presentation Skill 5

A stage is complete when every skill in it has been confirmed by a master.
