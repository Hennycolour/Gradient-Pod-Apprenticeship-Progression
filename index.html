Apprentice progression, made visible

A record that proves what an apprentice can do.

Apprenticeship should leave a readable trail: the skills practised, the hours put in, and the masters who confirmed the work. This is the record that closes the gap between learning and recognition.

Create account Learn the programme

Every entry is tied to a workshop, a stage, and a confirming master so the record is useful to apprentices, owners, and employers alike.

How the programme works

1. Learn the skill

Each apprentice records the practical work they complete in the workshop, alongside the date and the hours involved.

2. Confirm it

A master reviews the evidence and signs the entry, turning a memory into a verifiable milestone.

3. Move on

Confirmed stages build a consistent record that can travel with the apprentice from one workshop to the next.
