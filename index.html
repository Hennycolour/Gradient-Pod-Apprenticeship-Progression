How the record is kept

Three steps, repeated every time an apprentice moves forward.

  1. The apprentice logs the work

    Hours at the machine, the skill practised, and a short reflection in their own words.

  2. The master confirms it

    Nothing enters the record on the apprentice's word alone. A master signs off each skill.

  3. The record travels

    A confirmed skill belongs to the apprentice — across workshops, cities and years.
